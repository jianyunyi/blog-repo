# Australia TFN: Apply Online for Free — Pick the Right Route Before You Start

**One-line takeaway:** An Australian Tax File Number (TFN) is a 9-digit number, free to apply for, and yours for life. Without one, your employer must withhold tax at a higher rate. But there are three different application routes, and picking the wrong one wastes weeks: citizens go through myID, foreign passport holders in Australia use the IAR online system, and everyone else goes through Australia Post or a paper form.

## What you need

- First, identify your route: Australian citizen aged 15 or older with an Australian passport / foreign passport holder currently in Australia with a work-rights visa / other Australian resident
- For the foreign passport route: the passport linked to your visa, your visa grant number or TRN, and an Australian postal address where mail can actually reach you
- Timing expectations: the myID route for citizens is often instant; the ATO's stated window for other routes is within 28 days of receiving a complete application

## Steps

### Step 1: Start on the ATO's application page and confirm two facts: free, and for life

![ATO Australian residents TFN application page: any age, free to apply](img/2026-10-29-tfn/step1.png)

Open the ATO's TFN application page. Its headline facts: Australian residents can apply at any age, and it is free to apply. A TFN is your personal reference number in the tax and superannuation systems — it stays with you for life, across jobs and name changes. Anyone charging a "TFN application fee" is charging you to fill in a free form. Do not pay.

### Step 2: Foreign passport holders in Australia apply online through IAR — being in Australia is a hard requirement

![ATO foreign passport holders TFN application page: IAR online channel](img/2026-10-29-tfn/step2.png)

If you are physically in Australia, hold a foreign passport, and have a visa with work rights (including working holiday, student, permanent resident, or indefinite-stay visas), use the ATO's Individual Auto Registration (IAR) online system: confirm that you are in Australia, hold a foreign passport, and hold a work-rights visa, then enter your passport number exactly as printed, your date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY), your visa type, and your visa grant number. The ATO verifies your visa electronically with the Department of Home Affairs, so you send no physical documents. Your TFN is then posted to your Australian address, within 28 days per the ATO.

**Say this plainly:** IAR requires you to be in Australia. If you are outside Australia or hold no work-rights visa, you cannot use this online route — the alternative is the paper application for people living outside Australia (form plus certified copies of identity documents, posted to the ATO, same 28-day window). Ignore any ad promising an "instant online TFN from abroad."

### Step 3: The other routes, and who gets your TFN afterwards

![2026 TFN route comparison: myID citizens, IAR foreign passports, Australia Post interview, paper form](img/2026-10-29-tfn/step3.png)

- **Australian citizens aged 15 or older:** Set up the myID app to Strong identity strength (Australian passport plus a citizenship certificate, driver licence, learner permit, or Medicare card), then sign in to myGov, choose Continue with Digital ID, and complete the application. In most cases you receive your TFN straight away; otherwise allow up to 28 days.
- **Other Australian residents (no Strong myID):** Complete the online form, then attend an interview at a participating Australia Post outlet within 30 days with original identity documents, or visit a Services Australia centre, or post paper form NAT 1432.
- **After you receive it:** Give your TFN almost exclusively to your employer, your bank, and your super fund. If you lose it, do not apply again — one person has exactly one TFN for life. Find it in myGov's ATO online services, the ATO app, a notice of assessment, or a super statement.

## Notes, costs, and pitfalls

- **Cost:** $0 to apply, start to finish. A TFN itself creates no tax liability — it is an identifier; tax only arises when you have income to report.
- **The cost of not having one:** Without a TFN, your employer must withhold more tax — third-party summaries of the ATO position put it at 47% of wages (45% for foreign residents) until you supply the number. Over-withheld tax is recoverable when you lodge a return, but the cash-flow hit lasts for months, so apply before your first pay cycle if you can.
- **TFN vs. ABN:** They are different numbers. Sole traders need a TFN before they can get an Australian Business Number (ABN).
- **Scam check:** The ATO is a government agency — there is no "shutdown" risk. The real risk is impersonation: the ATO will not text you asking for your TFN or bank password. Delete such messages.
- **Pitfalls:** ① One wrong digit in your passport number fails the Home Affairs check — verify character by character before submitting. ② A postal address that cannot receive mail means a lost TFN letter and a reissue process. ③ Never apply twice. ④ Do not share your TFN beyond employer, bank, and super fund.
