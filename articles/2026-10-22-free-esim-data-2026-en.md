# Free eSIM Data in 2026: Every $0 Offer That Still Works (Tested & Verified)

> **TL;DR:** Stop googling "Airalo free trial" — it was discontinued in 2025. I checked every free eSIM data offer still alive in October 2026: Nomad's 1GB/3-day new-user trial, GigSky's 100MB/7-day trial (plus a Visa cardholder bonus), and Firsty's ad-supported free tier that never expires. Claim steps, data caps, expiry rules, and the catches — all $0, no credit card, about 10 minutes on your phone.

## Before you start (check these first)

1. **An eSIM-compatible phone**: iPhone XS and later, Samsung Galaxy S20 series and later, Pixel 3 and later. Check your exact model against each provider's compatibility list.
2. **No carrier lock** on your phone — locked phones block third-party eSIM activation.
3. **Stable Wi-Fi**: the eSIM profile must be downloaded while you're online. Install it at home, not after you land with no connection.
4. **An email address** for account signup.
5. All three main offers below cost **$0** with **no credit card required** (the Visa/Mastercard bonus tiers only run an eligibility check — no charge).

## Step 1: Nomad — free 1GB / 3 days, the most generous no-strings trial

This is the biggest genuinely free trial on the market right now: **1GB of high-speed data, valid 3 days, across 76 popular destinations** (US, UK, Japan, South Korea, most of Europe), and it **supports hotspot tethering**.

![Nomad's official free-trial page: new users can redeem a free 1GB trial eSIM](img/2026-10-22-esim-free-data/step1.png)

**How to claim (in the app, ~5 minutes):**

1. Download the **Nomad eSIM** app (iOS / Android).
2. Sign up with your email — you must be a **new user**.
3. Find the **Free Trial** entry on the home screen and tap to redeem.
4. Install the eSIM in one tap (or scan the QR code), and enable data roaming for it.
5. It activates automatically when you connect to a supported network at your destination. Install **before your trip**, but no earlier than 15 days out (it auto-activates 15 days after installation).

**The fine print** (from Nomad's official trial terms):
- 1GB high-speed data, 3 days, 76 destinations, no credit card.
- One per person. And note this: **once you redeem the trial, you lose eligibility for other first-time promotions** — so don't claim it casually if a bigger welcome deal matters to you.
- When the trial ends, data simply stops. **No auto-renewal, no surprise charges.** You can top up the same eSIM in the app if you want to keep going.

## Step 2: GigSky — free 100MB / 7 days, no card; Visa holders get a bonus

GigSky is a full MVNO (it partners directly with local carriers rather than reselling), and its free trial comes in two tiers:

![GigSky's official free-trial breakdown: 100MB/7 days up to 3GB/15 days for Visa cardholders](img/2026-10-22-esim-free-data/step2.png)

**Tier 1: everyone (no card)**
- **100MB / 7 days** across 125+ countries — added automatically when you register in the app.
- Heading to Europe? There's a dedicated **500MB / 7-day** Europe trial (42 countries).

**Tier 2: eligible Visa cardholders (card verified, never charged)**
- **Visa Infinite** (issued anywhere): 7 days of unlimited data + 30% off further plans.
- Other eligible Visa cards (including debit and prepaid): 3 days of unlimited data + 20% off.
- Visa Infinite / Signature **issued in the Americas**: **1–3GB / 15 days** (3GB in most of Latin America, 1GB for Canadian cards) + 20–30% off.

**How to claim:**
1. Download the **GigSky** app, create your account — the base 100MB trial lands automatically.
2. Have a Visa card? Go to **Offers** in the app → tap the Visa banner → enter your card number for a one-time eligibility check (no charge, ever).
3. Install the eSIM once. GigSky's profile is **reusable across trips and countries** — buy future plans in the app with no reinstall.

## Step 3: Firsty — a free tier that never expires (ad-supported, 176 countries)

The two above are one-time trials. Firsty is the **only long-term free tier**: Firsty Free costs €0 — watch a short ad to unlock **20MB** (up to 1 Mbps), stackable, up to **360MB per day**, in 176 countries.

![Firsty's homepage: FREE €0 forever tier — watch an ad, unlock free mobile data](img/2026-10-22-esim-free-data/step3.png)

**How to claim:**
1. Download the **Firsty** app and sign up (**phone-number verification required**).
2. Install the eSIM once — one profile works worldwide.
3. Watch a short ad in the app; 20MB lands in your balance. Top up with another ad before you run dry.

**Know its limits before you rely on it:**
- Speed is 256 Kbps–1 Mbps: **fine for messaging, email, maps, and ride-hailing** — useless for video calls, streaming, or photo uploads.
- **No hotspot on the Free tier** (hotspot needs Classic from €0.98/GB or Unlimited from €2/day).
- Signup requires phone verification, and some dialing codes are excluded (reported for parts of Africa and India in 2026 — check yours at signup).
- Users reported in August 2026 that a app update made the free tier harder to use (ads not loading). Practical tip from real users: **don't wait until your data is fully gone** to watch the next ad — and if you're offline with zero balance, you'll need Wi-Fi to reload.

- **Roamless**: free eSIM install for new users with **up to 500MB of test data**; the eSIM itself **never expires**. A solid permanent backup line across 200+ countries.

- **Monty eSIM (no free offer)**: verified — it has **no free trial or free data at all**, only a $3-each referral program and 10% loyalty cashback. Ignore outdated tutorials claiming otherwise.
- **Jetpac Eterna**: marketed as "lifetime free" (100MB per trip, up to 4×/year, plus free WhatsApp/Maps/Uber/Grab) — but the October 2026 limited re-release has **regional restrictions**; check the official page for your region before counting on it.
- **Opera for Android**: built-in eSIM with **3GB free, 3-day validity, 47 countries** — but free redemption is currently limited to users registering in 10 countries (US, UK, Germany, France, UAE, Brazil, Netherlands, Sweden, Norway, Poland).

## Costs, caveats, and hard lessons

**Cost**: every free tier above is genuinely $0. Visa/Mastercard bonus tiers only verify eligibility — no charge. Trials end without auto-renewal (stated explicitly by Nomad and GigSky).

**Caveats**:
1. **Free data is "landing insurance," not a travel plan.** 100MB ≈ an hour of map navigation plus texting; 1GB covers 2–3 days of light use. Use it to get online on arrival, then buy a real plan.
2. **Expiry is short**: Nomad 3 days, GigSky 7 days, iRoamly just 1 day. Install early — but not too early (Nomad auto-activates 15 days after install).
3. **One per person.** Don't try bulk signups to farm trials. Cautionary tale: Giffgaff disconnected prepaid users with extended/permanent use outside the UK in 2026 — abusing free offers gets accounts killed.
4. **Free tiers are data-only**: no number, no SMS. Keep your home SIM active in dual-SIM mode for calls and 2FA codes.
5. **Install on Wi-Fi.** The eSIM profile download needs internet — do it at home or at the hotel, not at the arrivals hall with no connection.
6. **Firsty's speed cap is real.** At sub-1 Mbps it's a messaging-and-maps tool. If your trip needs video calls, buy a paid tier instead of fighting the free one.

**Bottom line**: in 2026, free eSIM data still exists but the menu is short — Nomad's 1GB is the most useful, GigSky's 100MB is the easiest (with a Visa bonus if you have one), and Firsty's free tier is the only one you can keep using trip after trip. Airalo's and Monty's "free" offers are gone or never existed — close those old tutorial tabs.
