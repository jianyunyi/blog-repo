# No Phone Number, No Card: Tuta's Free Encrypted Email (1 GB) in Three Steps

**One-line takeaway:** If you want a separate encrypted side mailbox, Tuta's free plan gives you 1 GB, no phone number at signup, and encryption that covers even subject lines — the price is that you must live inside Tuta's own apps.

## What you need

- A browser or phone that can run Tuta's official app (web, Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, Android)
- Acceptance of the encrypted-email trade-off: security in exchange for convenience. This is not a Gmail clone.

## Steps

### Step 1: Go to the official site and pick the free plan

![Tuta homepage](img/2026-10-27-tuta/step1.png)

At tuta.com, choose the Free plan: 1 GB of storage, no card required. The free plan includes encrypted mail plus an encrypted calendar. Free address endings include @tutamail.com, @tutanota.com, @tutanota.de, @tuta.io, and @keemail.me (follow the actual dropdown at signup).

### Step 2: Sign up without a phone number — and guard your password and recovery code

![Tuta 2026 review, prices rechecked 8 October 2026](img/2026-10-27-tuta/step2.png)

A review that rechecked Tuta's own prices on 8 October 2026 confirms: the free plan is 1 GB, signup needs no phone number and no personal ID, and encryption covers more than most rivals — message bodies, subject lines, contacts, calendars, and the search index — with servers in Germany under GDPR. Save the recovery code offline at signup: the classic encrypted-mailbox problem is that a lost password is very hard to recover.

### Step 3: Live on the free plan for a week before paying anything

![Tuta review: free-plan limits and paid plans](img/2026-10-27-tuta/step3.png)

Paid-plan figures (consistent across 2026 reviews): Revolutionary around €3/month billed annually with 20 GB and custom domains; Legend around €8/month billed annually with 500 GB. Test three things on the free plan first: does mail to and from outside addresses work, do mobile push notifications arrive promptly, and is 1 GB enough for you? Pay only if all three pass.

## Costs, caveats, and pitfalls

- **Cost:** Free plan €0 with 1 GB; Revolutionary about €3/month, Legend about €8/month (annual billing; monthly may cost more — trust the checkout page).
- **Biggest gotcha: no IMAP/SMTP.** You cannot use Apple Mail, Outlook, or Thunderbird. Only Tuta's own web and apps work. That is a design choice, not a bug — if you cannot accept it, do not sign up.
- **No PGP either:** Tuta uses its own end-to-end encryption system, not PGP. Encrypted mail to non-Tuta users goes through its own password-link method.
- **New accounts can face approval delays:** Reviews note free accounts occasionally take time to approve. If mail does not arrive at first, wait and check spam before creating a pile of accounts.
- **Do not make it your main inbox:** 1 GB fills fast. Keep heavy attachments and newsletters in your main mailbox; use Tuta as a side mailbox for outreach and sensitive signups.
