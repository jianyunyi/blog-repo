# Get a Free UK Shipping Address: forward2me Parcel Forwarding, From £9.99 for Light Parcels

**One-line takeaway:** If you shop on UK-only stores but live elsewhere, a free forward2me account gives you a UK address — you only pay when a parcel actually ships onward.

## What you need

- An email address that can receive a verification message
- Parcels, not letters — forward2me forwards packages, it is not a mail-scanning mailbox service
- Your final delivery country in mind: shipping cost and import tax depend on it

## Steps

### Step 1: Sign up free and get your UK address

![forward2me Lite light-parcel service page](img/2026-10-27-forward2me/step1.png)

Signing up and getting the address is free. For light parcels, the official Lite terms are: under 2 kg and under £50 in value, shipped anywhere in the world from £9.99 untracked. Check your parcel fits that box before you order.

### Step 2: Order from UK stores using that address, then wait for the warehouse notice

![forward2me how-it-works explainer](img/2026-10-27-forward2me/step2.png)

The flow is four steps: sign up for the address → check out at UK retailers with that address → get notified when the parcel reaches the warehouse → log in, pick a courier, pay shipping, and have it sent to your real address. The official explainer is explicit: signup is free and you pay nothing until you forward something; parcels store free for 30 days, then £0.50 per parcel per day, and combine & repack costs £2 per parcel.

### Step 3: Price the whole trip before you ship — not just the freight

![forward2me third-party review, updated 1 October 2026](img/2026-10-27-forward2me/step3.png)

A third-party review updated 1 October 2026 confirms forward2me is an established forwarder with addresses in the UK, Germany, Japan, and Türkiye plus a tax-free Guernsey warehouse — but **no US address**. If you need a US address, use a different service. Before shipping, run the official shipping calculator for your weight, size, and destination, and budget import duties separately: your local customs authority charges those, and they are not included in forwarding freight.

## Costs, caveats, and pitfalls

- **Cost structure:** Account and address are free. You pay forwarding freight, optional combine & repack, storage after 30 free days, and destination duties.
- **It is not a mailing address:** Do not send bank cards or tax letters to a parcel forwarder. Letter handling needs a mailbox service with mail processing (see the Planet Express piece from 24 October); do not mix the two.
- **Keep the recipient name consistent:** Use the same name at checkout as on your forward2me account so the warehouse can match the parcel to you (unverified detail — follow the warehouse prompts in practice).
- **Restricted goods:** The warehouse checks whether contents can legally ship to your country. Confirm your item is not restricted before you buy.
- **No shutdown red flags found:** The October 2026 review still rates it as an established, reliable option, but no forwarder promises permanently cheap freight — price heavy or bulky parcels before committing.
