# Local USD, GBP & EUR Accounts Without Leaving Home: Opening an Airwallex Multi-Currency Business Account Online

> One-line takeaway: Airwallex lets a company open "local" collection accounts in the US, UK, EU, Hong Kong, Australia and more — your American clients pay via ACH in dollars, British clients via Faster Payments in pounds, EU clients via SEPA in euros — all on local rails, no SWIFT middlemen. Account opening is free, the entry plan costs $0/month, and the whole process runs 100% online with approval in 1–3 business days. The catches: businesses only (no personal accounts), and KYC is real — sloppy paperwork gets you stuck.

## The bottom line: still possible in 2026?

Yes. Airwallex's official pricing pages (checked 2026-10-23) state it plainly: **the Explore plan is free with no account opening fee**, and Global Accounts can receive and hold 20+ currencies. But three hard gates up front:

1. **Businesses only**: per Airwallex's own FAQ — "Airwallex currently only serves business customers; personal accounts are not offered." No registered entity, no account.
2. **It's compliance onboarding, not instant signup**: expect a genuine KYC/KYB review — incorporation documents, director/shareholder IDs, proof of business activity.
3. **It's not a bank**: Airwallex is a licensed fintech (electronic money institution) in each jurisdiction it operates in — no traditional deposit insurance (the UK entity, for instance, is not covered by the FSCS). The one-star reviews on Trustpilot are a warning against parking all your operating cash with a single provider (more below).

## What you need before you start

1. **A registered company** in one of Airwallex's ~69 supported jurisdictions (US, UK, EU, Singapore, Hong Kong, Australia, Canada and more — available features and currencies vary by where you're incorporated).
2. **Company registration documents**: certificate of incorporation / business registration (Hong Kong: Business Registration Certificate + Certificate of Incorporation + Articles of Association).
3. **Director & UBO IDs**: passport or national ID, plus proof of residential address dated within the last 3 months (utility bill, bank statement) for every director and ultimate beneficial owner.
4. **Proof of business**: company website, sales contracts or invoices — to show you're a real operating business. This is where most applications stall.
5. **A business email**: use your company domain. Multiple reviews and Airwallex itself note that personal email addresses can trigger manual compliance review.

Cost: **$0 to open, $0/month on the Explore plan.** Paid tiers exist (e.g., from A$29/month or £19/month in some regions unless you meet deposit/balance thresholds; Accelerate with a dedicated account manager is the top tier). FX runs at the interbank rate plus 0.5% for major currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, SGD, HKD, CNY, CAD, CHF, NZD, JPY) and plus 1% for the rest.

Before anything, open Airwallex's official pricing page for your region and confirm the free tier exists with your own eyes — don't let a middleman upsell you:

Three things to read off it:

1. **Explore = free**: Global Business Account, receiving and holding 20+ currencies, batch transfers to 120+ countries/regions, interbank FX rates, 5 free spend-management users, unlimited Visa corporate cards. Enough for most early-stage cross-border teams.
2. **Region matters**: what's free and which features are included varies by your company's domicile — compare the pricing page for your region, not someone else's screenshot.
3. **Monthly free ≠ everything free**: FX conversions, SWIFT transfers, extra spend users and card usage carry per-transaction fees. Read the fee schedule, not just the headline.

## Step 2: Register online and upload your business documents

Go to Airwallex's site, pick the region matching your company's registration, and hit "Get started." The whole flow is online — no branch visits:

![Airwallex account opening page: start online with your business email](img/2026-10-23-airwallex/step2.png)

Four steps, roughly 5 minutes of your time for the application itself:

1. **Sign up**: business email, password, email verification, and turn on two-factor authentication (2FA).
2. **Enter company details**: legal name, registration number, operating address, industry classification, expected monthly volume — **names must match character-for-character across your company registry record, IDs, and application**. Mismatches are the #1 rejection cause.
3. **Upload KYC/KYB documents**: incorporation papers, director and UBO IDs with address proofs, business evidence (website / contracts / invoices). PDF, JPEG, PNG or BMP under 10 MB each; blurry scans are the classic trigger for RFI (request for information) delays.
4. **Wait for review**: official line is 1–3 business days. Respond to any follow-up document requests fast.

> Scam warning: searches turn up dozens of "buy a verified Airwallex account" listings. **Never buy one.** The original owner can recover it, funds can be frozen at any moment, and you're buying into someone else's fraud liability. The only legitimate route is applying with your own company on the official site.

## Step 3: Create Global Accounts and collect your local account details

Once approved, head to Wallet > Global Accounts in the dashboard, pick a region and currency, and you get collection accounts **with genuine local bank details** — your clients pay you like they'd pay any local company. Airwallex's official docs lay out the coverage like this:

![Airwallex official docs: Global Accounts supported regions, currencies and local clearing networks](img/2026-10-23-airwallex/step3.png)

The highlights (per the official docs, updated 2025-08-15 — re-check at application time):

- **United States (USD)**: ACH routing number + Fedwire routing number + SWIFT; local rails include ACH, Fedwire, FedNow and RTP.
- **United Kingdom (GBP)**: account number + sort code + IBAN; Faster Payments / BACS / CHAPS.
- **Europe (EUR via SEPA)**: Estonia, Germany, the Netherlands and Denmark offer EUR accounts on SEPA / SEPA Instant (some restricted by customer domicile — e.g., Netherlands accounts aren't available to Hong Kong/China-domiciled customers).
- **Hong Kong**: CNY/HKD via ACH / RTGS / FPS; EUR/USD via RTGS; plus AUD, GBP, JPY and more.
- **Plus**: Australia (AUD), Singapore (SGD via FAST/GIRO/MEPS), Canada (CAD via EFT/Interac), Japan (JPY), New Zealand (NZD), Indonesia (IDR), Mexico (MXN via SPEI), Israel (ILS), Poland (PLN), UAE (AED), Malaysia (MYR).
- **SWIFT fallback**: accounts in the US, UK, Hong Kong, Singapore and Denmark also accept SWIFT transfers in 22 currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, CNY, HKD, AUD, …).

Incoming funds land in your multi-currency wallet: hold them in the original currency (e.g., keep dollars to pay dollar suppliers later) or convert at the interbank rate. Airwallex reports ~93% of local-rail payments arrive same-day.

## Costs, limits, and gotchas — cheat sheet

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Account opening | $0 |
| Monthly fee | $0 on Explore; paid tiers from ~A$29/£19/mo in some regions (waivable with qualifying deposits/balances) |
| FX | Interbank rate +0.5% (majors: USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, SGD, HKD, CNY, CAD, CHF, NZD, JPY), +1% others |
| Local-rail transfers | $0 transfer fees to 120+ countries/regions; SWIFT payouts to 200+ countries (per-transfer fee) |
| Currencies | 20+ currencies receivable and holdable; local collection in 70+ countries/regions |
| Documents | Incorporation/registration certificate, director + UBO IDs with address proofs, proof of business (site/contracts/invoices), business email |
| Approval time | 1–3 business days; unclear documents trigger RFI delays |
| Personal accounts | ❌ Not supported — businesses only |
| Address proof | Required (director/UBO residential address proof, < 3 months old) |
| Risks | Trustpilot 2.8–2.9/5 from 2,500+ reviews (Sep–Oct 2026 data): one-stars cluster on compliance-driven account/fund freezes, delayed receipts, slow support; **not a bank — no deposit insurance**, don't keep all operating cash in one place |
| Good for | Cross-border e-commerce collection, SaaS subscriptions, multi-currency indie businesses, paying overseas suppliers/staff |

The one sentence the one-star reviews keep repeating: **getting approved is easy; staying approved is the real test.** Before your first large incoming payment, have contracts, invoices and source-of-funds proof ready; keep company details identical across your registry record, IDs and Airwallex profile. When a risk model trips, support is email-based and slow (only the top tier gets a named account manager) — so don't bet your only revenue stream on one provider. A Wise or WorldFirst account alongside costs nothing and buys you sleep.
