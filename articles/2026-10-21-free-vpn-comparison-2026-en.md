## What You Need

- A computer or phone (Windows / macOS / Android / iOS)
- An email address (for sign-up)
- A network that can reach the VPN providers' official sites (you only need it to download the client)

## Step 3: How to Claim Each One

The claiming path is the same for all five: **official site → Pricing → Free tier → download the client → register**.

1. **Proton VPN** (protonvpn.com): Swiss company, no-logs policy with annual independent audits. Free tier: 1 device, ~10 auto-assigned countries. Best for: long-term light use without watching a data meter.
2. **Windscribe** (windscribe.com): Canadian company. **Verify your email at sign-up** or you only get 2 GB/month. Unlimited devices on the free tier — unique in this list. Best for: multi-device users who want to pick countries.
3. **PrivadoVPN** (privadovpn.com): Swiss company. 10 GB/30 days, 13 self-selected locations, no public third-party audit, claims free-tier streaming works. Best for: anyone willing to gamble on free streaming.
4. **hide.me** (hide.me): Malaysian company, no-logs audit in 2024. Works without registration, but throttled to ~1 Mbps the whole way — fine for browsing and email, forget video. Best for: quick one-off use without handing over an email.
5. **TunnelBear** (tunnelbear.com): Canadian company, annual Cure53 audits, the best onboarding for first-timers. 2 GB/month is emergency-only. Best for: absolute VPN beginners.

## Step 4: Universal Rules for Free VPNs

1. **Demand an independent audit.** A free VPN's business model must be "paying users subsidize the free tier," and it should dare to hire third-party auditors for its no-logs policy. Of the five above, PrivadoVPN is the only one with no public audit — weigh that yourself.
2. **Read the business model.** If the free tier is fully featured, there's no paid version, and the lights stay on via ad popups, you're the product. Free tiers are funnels toward paid plans — feature cuts are the norm. Accept that.
3. **Avoid the blacklist:** Hola, UrbanVPN, and SuperVPN have track records of selling data or shipping malware. Don't install them, however cheap.
4. **Never log into your bank or type payment passwords over a free VPN.** The security margin on a free tier is simply lower than paid — use a trusted network for sensitive actions.

## Notes, Costs, Gotchas

- Cost: all five free tiers are $0; paid upgrades are optional.
- "Unlimited" almost always hides a cap — Proton limits countries, hide.me limits speed. Read before you pick.
- One device is the norm on free tiers (Windscribe excepted); for phone + laptop either log in on one, or pick Windscribe.
- Free-tier VPN IPs are heavily flagged by platforms — don't expect streaming unlocks or SMS verification codes to work.
