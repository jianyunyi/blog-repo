# Firsty Free eSIM: Watch an Ad, Get 20 MB — Land Connected, Not Stranded

**One-line takeaway:** The worst moment abroad is landing with no data and no plan yet. Firsty's free tier exists for exactly that gap: install one eSIM, watch a short ad, get 20 MB at 1 Mbps, and stack up to 360 MB a day — no credit card, no contract. It covers WhatsApp, maps, email, and ride-hailing, the four things that save a landing. It will not stream video, share a hotspot, or serve as a long-term number.

## What you need

- A phone that supports eSIM (recent iPhones, Pixels, and Samsung flagships generally do; older models and dual-physical-SIM models without eSIM cannot use this at all — skip this guide if yours is one)
- Wi-Fi before departure: the eSIM profile must be installed while you still have internet — do not wait until you land with no connection
- A phone number (for registration; the free tier needs no credit card)
- The expectation that the free tier runs at 1 Mbps in 20 MB slices — it is emergency connectivity, not an unlimited plan

## Steps

### Step 1: Read the official site and confirm what Firsty Free actually gives you

![Firsty homepage: one eSIM across 170+ countries](img/2026-10-30-firsty/step1.png)

Open Firsty's site and note its core offer: one eSIM across 170+ countries (some pages say 170, others 176 — treat the in-app coverage as the ground truth), no new card per border crossed, no contract, no roaming bill. It sits alongside your physical SIM as a dual-SIM setup: the physical SIM keeps calls and texts, Firsty handles mobile data. Settle that division of labor before departure and landing gets much calmer.

### Step 2: Verify the free tier's exact rules — 20 MB, 1 Mbps, 360 MB a day

![Firsty Free plan page: ad-based data, up to 360 MB per day](img/2026-10-30-firsty/step2.png)

Per Firsty's free-plan page, checked at the end of October 2026:

- **One ad = 20 MB at 1 Mbps, valid 7 days.** 20 MB covers dozens of WhatsApp messages, a few map lookups, and several emails — not short video.
- **Balance cap 240 MB:** watch up to 12 ads in a row to fill a 240 MB balance; use some data before you can watch more ads.
- **Daily cap 360 MB:** at most 18 ads, 360 MB per 24 hours; after that, wait for the next cycle or upgrade.
- **No credit card:** the free tier never asks for one — ads are its revenue.
- **Paid tiers for reference (check in-app prices):** Classic from €0.98/GB (0.5–50 GB packs, full speed, 30-day validity), Unlimited from €2/day (5 GB at full speed daily, then 512 Kbps, hotspot included).

Version warning: reviews from 2024 describe a "60-minute free session, then watch an ad to continue" model. The rules have since changed to MB-based accounting — do not follow a 2024 guide against today's app; trust the free-plan page.

### Step 3: Install the app and the eSIM, turn on roaming — all on Wi-Fi, before departure

![Firsty app store page: free mobile data, watch an ad to get online](img/2026-10-30-firsty/step3.png)

Download Firsty from the App Store or Google Play (store-page figures: 1.2M+ users, 4.8/5 on Trustpilot — indicative only), then follow the in-app flow:

1. Register (phone-number verification). The app installs the eSIM profile automatically — no QR code needed.
2. In your phone's settings, set the Firsty eSIM as the mobile-data line, and turn on data roaming for that eSIM — the roaming switch is mandatory for an eSIM to attach to a local network. Leave roaming off on your physical SIM to avoid accidental roaming charges.
3. While still on Wi-Fi, watch one ad and confirm 20 MB appears in your balance before you leave. Installing an eSIM after landing, with no connection, is a dead loop — do it now.

### Step 4: After landing, use it for the "four lifelines" only, and stay inside its limits

![Firsty eSIM explainer: how an eSIM works](img/2026-10-30-firsty/step4.png)

Once Firsty data is on after landing, do four things: WhatsApp/iMessage to say you arrived, maps to reach the hotel, email to collect confirmations, and a ride-hailing app to get a car. Per Firsty's own pages, ride-hailing apps get favorable treatment on the free tier (some pages say Uber runs without ad-watching; treat in-app behavior as the ground truth). When data runs out, watch another ad for 20 MB more; when the balance is full at 240 MB, use some before stacking more.

The overreach list (the free tier cannot do these — do not force it): streaming video, video calls, large downloads, hotspot sharing with a laptop (hotspot is a paid-tier feature), or using it as a long-term keep-alive number (it is data-only, with no phone number to preserve — for number-keeping, see this series' eSIM keep-alive guides).

## Notes, costs, and pitfalls

- **Cost:** Free is €0, no card; Classic from €0.98/GB, Unlimited from €2/day, per in-app prices.
- **Track record:** Firsty is a Dutch startup (Bamboo Connected) that has run its free tier since 2024 with no public record of mass bans or sudden free-tier removal; but its free rules have changed (60-minute sessions → MB accounting), and its own pages disagree on country counts (170 vs 176) — treat in-app coverage and rules as authoritative, not marketing numbers.
- **Pitfalls:** ① Phones without eSIM (including dual-physical-SIM models) cannot install it — skip outright. ② Installing the eSIM only after landing, with no Wi-Fi, leaves you stuck — install before departure. ③ Forgetting the eSIM's data-roaming switch: if it will not connect, check that switch first. ④ At 1 Mbps, download offline maps in advance; web timeouts are a speed issue, not a fault. ⑤ The free tier is data-only with no phone number — it cannot receive verification codes or keep a number alive.
