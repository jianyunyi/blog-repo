# Student AI Plans With Hard Deadlines: ChatGPT Plus Ends October 31, Google Ends December 31

**One-line summary:** The most urgent free AI plan this week is ChatGPT Plus for students — claims close on October 31, 2026, four months worth $80 — while Google's student plan runs a full free year but closes December 31. Both need student verification and a payment method, and their cancellation traps are opposites.

## What you need

- Current college or graduate-student status (ChatGPT's offer is limited to eligible US degree-granting colleges, full- or part-time; Google's is US students for AI Pro and students in 140+ other markets for AI Plus)
- A personal account (do not make a school-managed Workspace account the subscription owner — you lose it at graduation)
- A payment method (both offers require one up front; nothing is charged during the free period, **but not cancelling means automatic billing when it ends**)
- To be 18 or older (explicit for Google's offer)

> Cold water first: a review re-checked on October 20, 2026 found that **no major AI paid plan is free for everyone**. Free routes exist only for specific groups. Any post promising "free Plus/Pro for all" is an old offer, a referral trap or a phishing link.

## Step 1: Claim the urgent one first — ChatGPT Plus for students (deadline October 31, 2026)

![Student AI plans compared: deadlines and renewal prices](img/2026-10-26-ai-student/step1.png)

1. Open the ChatGPT student offer page (chatgpt.com/students/2026) in a browser and sign in to **the ChatGPT account you want upgraded** — the offer attaches to the signed-in account and cannot be moved.
2. Complete SheerID verification. Depending on your school, SheerID may confirm you from trusted records, ask for school single sign-on, or request documents such as a student ID, transcript or tuition receipt (upload only what the official flow asks for).
3. Return to the offer flow and finish activation. New subscribers add a payment method when prompted; nothing is charged for the four free months.
4. Before confirming, check three things: the free period is **four monthly billing periods**, the current charge is $0, and renewal afterwards is $20/month.
5. Two limits: one redemption per person, and Plus subscriptions billed through the App Store or Google Play **cannot** take this offer while they stay store-billed (switch to direct ChatGPT billing first, and confirm the consequences before doing so).

**Value:** 4 months × $20 = $80. On October 26 there are only 5 days left — claim today, because a failed verification needs time for document re-submission.

## Step 2: Claim Google's student plan (deadline December 31, 2026, free for 12 months)

![Student AI discounts compared: Google, ChatGPT and Copilot](img/2026-10-26-ai-student/step2.png)

1. Open the Gemini for Students page (gemini.google/students) or the Google One student page (one.google.com/ai-student) and click Claim Free Student Plan.
2. Sign in with a **personal Google Account** (not a school-managed account) and verify enrolment through SheerID.
3. Add a payment method and read the checkout summary: US version is **Google AI Pro, 12 months at $0, then $19.99/month**; in 140+ other markets it is **AI Plus, 12 months at $0, then $4.99/month or the local equivalent**.
4. Right after activation, open Google One membership settings and record five things: **plan name, activation date, renewal date, renewal price, cancellation path** — screenshot them. That record beats any guide later.
5. Do not confuse the two clocks: **December 31, 2026 is the deadline to redeem**; your 12 free months start on the day you redeem (redeem in October 2026 and you are free until October 2027), not on the August announcement date.

**Plan-name check (a lesson from the series' October 9 article):** outside the US you receive AI Plus, not Pro. Verify the plan name on your subscription page before telling anyone you got "Pro".

## Step 4: The cancellation table — set the calendar reminder before you forget

| Offer | Free period | Renewal | If you cancel early |
|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT Plus (students) | 4 months | $20/month | **Remaining free months are forfeited** — access runs only to the end of the current billing month. Do not cancel early; cancel inside month 4 |
| Google AI Pro / Plus (students) | 12 months | $19.99 / $4.99 per month | Early cancellation generally keeps access until the free period ends (confirm on your own account page) — you can switch off auto-renewal right after claiming |
| Codex $100 credit | Credits expire after 1 year | No auto-renewal | Unused credits simply expire; nothing is charged |

Do this today:

1. Set two calendar reminders now: one inside ChatGPT's 4th month, one inside Google's 11th month.
2. ChatGPT: **do not** cancel in month 1 (you lose the other 3 months). Cancel inside month 4: access runs to that month's end, then nothing is charged.
3. Google: once the subscription page checks out, you can cancel auto-renewal in Google One / Google Play subscription settings and keep using the free year to its end.
4. If a claim fails, check the three usual causes first — wrong account signed in, school-managed account as the subscription owner, or age / school-country mismatch — rather than retrying verification repeatedly or switching regions.

## Costs, limits and warnings

- **Costs:** $0 during the free periods; afterwards ChatGPT $20/month, Google Pro $19.99/month, Plus $4.99/month unless cancelled.
- **Never buy "student verification as a service":** SheerID verification must be done by the student. Handing your student identity and payment method to a stranger risks account bans and card fraud.
- **Do not hoard offer links:** third-party roundup links can point to expired campaigns. Before claiming, confirm the deadline and plan name on the official student page itself.
- **Not verified:** the deadlines and renewal prices here combine several third-party reviews from October 20–23, 2026 (each stating it checked the official pages); the author did not complete student verification for each offer. The Jio 18-month Google AI Pro offer (deadline October 29) is India Jio users only and is mentioned here for completeness, not as a main recommendation.
