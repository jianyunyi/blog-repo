# Planet Express: Get a Free US Address and Forward Packages Worldwide (Step-by-Step)

**One-line summary:** Planet Express's Free Mailbox gives you a real US street address with your own suite number for $0 — receiving mail and packages is free; you only pay a $2 mail-out fee per package plus shipping. This walkthrough covers sign-up, getting your address, receiving mail, and forwarding, step by step.

## What You Need

- An email address (Gmail works fine for sign-up)
- A way to pay in USD (card or PayPal) — sign-up requires a **$5 minimum deposit**, which stays as your account credit toward future shipping (wire transfers require $50 minimum)
- If you want to receive *letters* (bank cards, IRS tax letters): you'll need a notarized **USPS Form 1583** (a US legal requirement for mail-forwarding authorization)
- No US phone number, no travel needed — everything is done online

## Step 1: Check the Free Plan on the Official Site

Go to https://planetexpress.com/ and open the **Pricing** page. The key facts, straight from the official site:

- **Free Mailbox: $0/month** — free US receiving address, free receiving of letters and packages, 10 days of free storage per package, 2 free arrival photos per package, free disposal of unwanted packages
- **Premium: $10/month or $50/year** — tax-free-state (Oregon/Florida) address, 60 days of free storage (90 in Florida), package consolidation
- On **both** plans, every package shipped out incurs a **$2 mail-out/handling fee**, plus the carrier shipping cost (USPS, DHL, FedEx, Aramex available)

![Planet Express homepage: free sign-up and free plan overview](img/2026-10-24-planet-express/step1.png)

> Worth knowing: the free plan has no package consolidation and no tax-free-state address (the free default warehouse is in California, where online orders carry ~9.5% sales tax). If you only need to receive bank cards, letters, or forward the occasional small parcel, the free tier is enough.

## Step 2: Sign Up, Deposit $5, Get Your Dedicated US Address

Click **Sign Up**, enter your email and set a password. Then open your dashboard to find your address, which looks like:

```
Your Name / Suite PE-XXXXXX
17224 S Figueroa St
Gardena, CA 90248
United States
```

![Pricing page: Free $0 vs Premium $50/year compared](img/2026-10-24-planet-express/step2.png)

Three things to note:

1. **A $5 minimum deposit is required at sign-up** (stated in the official FAQ; $50 minimum for wire transfers). This is an anti-fraud measure, not a fee — it sits in your account balance, offsets future shipping costs, and can be refunded via PayPal following the official instructions.
2. **Always include your suite number** (the PE-prefixed ID) on every order — the warehouse uses it to match packages to your account. A package arriving without it gets stuck and needs manual claiming by email.
3. This is a real commercial street address, not a PO Box — it works with US retailers that refuse PO Box deliveries.

## Step 3: Use the Address at US Stores or Banks, and Receive

Example — buying from a US online store: at checkout, enter the address above as your shipping address, with your real name plus the suite number. When the parcel reaches the warehouse you'll get an email notification, and in your dashboard you can see the weight, dimensions, and 2 free arrival photos (box exterior + shipping label).

![How It Works page: order → warehouse receipt → dashboard actions → worldwide forwarding](img/2026-10-24-planet-express/step3.png)

**Receiving letters, bank cards, and tax letters (the key use case):**

- To receive *letter mail* you must first complete the **notarized USPS Form 1583**. Planet Express's official FAQ points to NotaryLive for online notarization.
- **Credit cards can be forwarded**: the official FAQ confirms this, subject to the same 1583 notarization.
- **Big caveat**: letter mail containing *address verification codes* from retailers (Amazon, eBay, Shopify, etc.) is **explicitly prohibited** — don't use this address for marketplace seller verification.

## Step 4: Choose a Shipping Method, Fill the Customs Declaration, Ship Out

Once a package arrives: in the dashboard select the package → pick a carrier → fill in the customs declaration yourself (item name, quantity, purchase price — declare honestly; an incorrect declaration that customs sends back costs $15 to amend) → optionally add insurance → pay and ship.

Money-saving tips:

- For small, light items (a few books, a T-shirt, a card), choose **USPS** — Planet Express is one of the few forwarders that lets small items go by USPS instead of forcing a courier, which keeps light-parcel shipping genuinely cheap.
- Consolidating several parcels into one shipment saves money — but **consolidation is a Premium-only feature**; free users ship parcels one by one.
- DHL and FedEx quotes include insurance; with USPS, consider adding a few dollars of insurance — uninsured lost packages are not compensated.
- The Oregon/Florida warehouses are sales-tax-free, but those are Premium addresses; the free California warehouse adds ~9.5% tax to orders — do the math on expensive items.

## Fees, Gotchas, and Things to Watch

| Item | Cost / rule | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free address | $0 | Genuinely free; suite number issued at sign-up |
| Sign-up deposit | $5 minimum | Account credit, offsets shipping / refundable |
| Mail-out fee | $2 per package | Charged on both free and paid plans |
| Free storage | 10 days | Then $0.01/lb/day |
| Extra photos | $2 for 3 general, $5 for 10 detailed | 2 arrival photos free |
| Consolidation | Not on free plan | Premium: $5 + $2 per package |
| Premium | $10/mo or $50/yr | 60-day storage (90 in FL) + tax-free-state address + consolidation |
| Letter receiving | Requires notarized USPS 1583 | Online notarization via NotaryLive |

**Gotcha list (all pulled from the official site and real reviews):**

1. **Quotes can "grow"**: the most common Trustpilot complaint is shipping costing more than estimated because of dimensional-weight billing. Run the official postage calculator before committing — light-but-bulky items billed by dimensional weight can be shockingly expensive.
2. **Some sellers blacklist forwarding addresses**: reviewers report orders cancelled when shipped to forwarding warehouses. Planet Express's suggested workaround is their Shop For Me assisted-purchase service ($5 service fee + 7%).
3. **Support is weekdays only** (Mon–Fri, plus Saturday mornings US Pacific time). Don't place urgent orders over the weekend.
4. **Prohibited items are strict**: standalone lithium batteries, e-liquids, cash/currency, prescription drugs, knives via certain carriers — the official FAQ has the full list.
5. **Set delivery expectations realistically**: economy USPS to most countries takes 2–4 weeks; use DHL/FedEx when it matters.
